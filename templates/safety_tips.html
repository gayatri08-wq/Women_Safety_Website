Personal Safety Tips

Practical reminders for everyday situations.

📱

Keep Your Phone Ready

Charge your phone before travelling and keep important contacts accessible.

📍

Share Your Journey

When appropriate, share your route or arrival plans with someone you trust.

🚕

Travel Carefully

Use trusted transport where possible and check the vehicle details before starting your journey.

👥

Stay Connected

Tell a trusted person if your plans change or you feel unsafe.

🔐

Protect Your Privacy

Share your live location only with people you trust. Review app permissions and account privacy settings.

🆘

Know How to Get Help

Learn how to contact local emergency services. In India, call 112 for an immediate emergency.

No safety measure can guarantee complete protection. If you are in danger, move to a safer place if possible and contact emergency services.
