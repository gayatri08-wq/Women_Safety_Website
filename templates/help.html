Emergency Helpline

Important helpline numbers for people in India.

If you face an immediate emergency, call 112. Confirm the correct service number and availability for your area.

112 — Emergency Response

For urgent police, fire or medical assistance.

Call 112

181 — Women Helpline

Women in distress may contact this service where available.

Call 181

108 — Ambulance

Medical emergency assistance where this service operates.

Call 108

Helpline availability may vary by state and service. These buttons open your device's calling app; they do not place a call automatically.
